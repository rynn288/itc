The Journey

Filipino food is all about balancing sour, salty, and sweet. Vinegar cuts through the fat, soy sauce and fish sauce bring the salt, and a bit of sugar or calamansi balances it out. Once you understand that, the rest of the food starts to make more sense.

This trip went from Manila to Iloilo and ended in Cebu. Each stop below is a real meal I had, with what it cost and if I would order it again.

Where the Food Came From

  • Manila - night market food and a late bowl of arroz caldo.
  • Pampanga - sisig on a sizzling plate.
  • Iloilo - La Paz batchoy in the morning.
  • Cebu - lechon by the roadside.

Dishes to Try

Here are three dishes I think everyone should try at least once.

Adobo

Meat cooked in vinegar, soy sauce, garlic, and bay leaf until the sauce thickens. Every family has their own version. This is a good dish to start with.

Sinigang

A sour soup made with tamarind or green mango. Good to eat when it's raining.

Sisig

Chopped pork and onions served on a hot sizzling plate with calamansi on top. Best eaten with friends.

Cook it at Home

This chicken adobo recipe takes about 40 minutes. The ingredients don't need to go in any specific order, but the steps do, so I used a bullet list for one and a numbered list for the other.

Chicken Adobo

Serves 4 40 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken thighs
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/2 cup vinegar
  • 1 head garlic, crushed
  • 1 tsp peppercorns
  • 3 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp brown sugar
  • 1 cup water

Steps

  1. Marinate the chicken in soy sauce, garlic, and peppercorns for 30 minutes.
  2. Sear the chicken in a hot pan until golden. Set aside.
  3. Add the marinade, vinegar, bay leaves, and water. Let it boil for 2 minutes without stirring.
  4. Add the chicken back, cover, and simmer for 25 minutes.
  5. Add the sugar and reduce the sauce until it thickens.
  6. Serve with rice.

Before You Go

A few things I wish I knew before this trip.

  • Rice is the main part of the meal, not a side dish.
  • Carinderias that are busy at lunch time are usually the good ones.
  • Ask for calamansi and chili on the side.
  • Try the dessert even if you don't know what it is.
